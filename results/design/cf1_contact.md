## Candidate A (recommended): Pre-Contact Transport (PCT)
Idea: residual reuse carries the residual in outcome coordinates, after the contact map; PCT carries the disturbance in command coordinates, before the contact map, then pushes it through a learned sharp hybrid plant map. For an observed outcome y the action-likelihood a -> p(y | H, a) is a translate of the disturbance density, and its location is the effective command that produced y; the counterfactual outcome is the one whose action-likelihood is the factual one shifted by a' - a.
Assumptions: channel assumption CH: F(H, a, eps) = C_H(a + eta), eta = q(H, eps) in R^p, C_H arbitrary measurable (non-injective, discontinuous, piecewise); physically a matched disturbance followed by any plant including contact resolution; general form C_H(m(H, a) + eta); C = identity recovers the additive class. Regular outcomes: a set where C_H is injective with disjoint image; collapsed outcomes otherwise (blocked, untouched, clipped). Not covered: disturbances acting on the object itself. CH has testable necessary conditions but cannot be confirmed from factual data (gauge theorem). No extra information for the blind variant; the default uses readout or proprioceptive coordinates of the actuated body.
Algorithm: Step 0 fit mu(H, a) by squared loss. Step 1 abduct the effective command: 1a anchor (default): u_hat' = y_A + mu_A(H, a') - mu_A(H, a) on actuated-body coordinates, with a factual independence check of the residual from a (HSIC, or R^2 < 0.02 regressing the squared residual on (H, a)); 1b location-family inverse dynamics without anchor: encoder e_phi(H, y) and shared noise shape nu_psi (mixture of 8 diagonal Gaussians after a learned linear map, scale floor 0.02), minimising -log nu_psi(e - a) + log Integral_A nu_psi(e - a') pi(a') da' over regular outcomes (24 x 24 grid for p = 2, 256 Monte Carlo points otherwise). Step 2 hybrid decoder: regress y on (H, u_hat) on regular outcomes (MLP 3 x 512 GELU, AdamW 1e-3, weight decay 1e-5, 10k steps, batch 512); collapse gate g(H, u) trained by marginal likelihood (regular: log(1 - g(H, u_hat)); collapsed: log mean_k g(H, a + eta_k), 64 draws). Step 3 counterfactual: regular factual y_hat(a') = C_theta(H, u_hat + a' - a) (collapsed expert where g > 0.5); collapsed factual: 1024 particles u_k = a + eta_k weighted by g, y_hat(a') = sum_k w_k C_theta(H, u_k + a' - a), plus mode probabilities and support set; the nested form y + C(u_hat + delta) - C(u_hat) hurt on contact coordinates and is off by default. Step 4 factual certificates: held-out R^2 of C_theta (equals PCT's counterfactual risk under CH); model-implied interaction ratio rho_hat per coordinate; residual reuse where rho_hat < 0.1, PCT otherwise. Hindsight planning: the authors' CEM on y_hat(a').
Candid novelty: ingredients known (inverse dynamics, mixture-of-experts decoders, abduction-action-prediction, input-disturbance models and unknown-input observers, control functions). Claimed new: likelihood-translation identification (T2) collapsing the gauge to a translation without bijectivity and through non-injective contact maps; location-family inverse dynamics with a prior-truncation normaliser and the collapsed-mode gate likelihood; proof that every outcome-space shift rule, mode-switched included, keeps order-one error across and within contact; the factual interaction certificate. A control engineer will call the core "an input-disturbance model with a learned plant".

Theorems.
- T1 (limits of outcome-space transport; proved, elementary): any rule y + c(H, a, a') has risk tr Var(Delta); on a mode-switch event of probability pi with contrast jump J the risk is at least pi(1 - pi)(J - 2r)^2, independent of duration; a mode-switched shift has risk E Var(Delta | modes), still order one when the within-mode response is nonlinear; if C collapses information, no injective readout makes action and disturbance add (so the authors' direction (ii) cannot fix contacts in outcome space).
- T2 (gauge collapse; proof sketch complete for A = R^p with a Lebesgue density): equal factual laws force x~(y) = x(y) - c and nu~ = nu(. + c); if the characteristic function of nu has no zeros, Wiener's Tauberian theorem gives C~ = C(. + c) almost everywhere, collapsed region included; hence all counterfactual laws coincide. Bounded A, rank-deficient disturbances and gain faults: sketch or conjecture.
- T3 (short-time bound across modes): with a globally L-Lipschitz projection (clipping, sliding, saturation) and an injective factual mode, PCT error <= 2 L C h^2 (proved); with jumps J across a surface crossed with density of order 1/h, risk <= 4 L^2 C^2 h^4 + c J^2 C h (sketch); residual reuse stays at pi(1 - pi) J^2.
- T4 (minimax separation; lower bound sketch, upper bound conditional): any estimator has risk of order J^2 h in the jump class; PCT attains it when the map is known or exactly identified.
- T5: for a collapsed factual the identified counterfactual set is sharp and the posterior mean is Bayes-optimal; PCT's risk is never above residual reuse or the outcome-blind oracle inside CH.

## Candidate B: scale-and-shift channel transport (novelty 0.12 alone)
Location-scale command channel u = a + s(H, a) eta; abduct eta_hat = (u_hat - a) / s(a), transport u' = a' + s(a') eta_hat, push through C; with linear C this is y' = mu(a') + (s(a') / s(a))(y - mu(a)) (the authors' open "rescale the residual with the command"). Kill test on hand_affine50/100 and cup dial configs: lambda = 1 counterfactual R^2 >= residual reuse + 30 and above the trained predictor; within 2 points at lambda = 0. Role: one section of the PCT paper.

## Candidate C: mode-switched residual reuse (novelty 0.05)
The authors' direction (iii) literally; T1(b) is a lower bound against it; with privileged contact labels block velocities give -13 / -23 / -41 at 60k and 1.0 / -3.8 / -30.5 at 355k. Mandatory baseline and motivating negative result.

## Recommendation of the designer
Run Candidate A's kill test with B as its gain-fault section and C as a baseline; decide on K1, K3 and K4. Reviewer should press on the tie with the anchor-residual-conditioned predictor on Push-T, the gap between exact structure and the learned decoder (velocities 30-60%), and the unproven blind variant under rank-deficient disturbances.
