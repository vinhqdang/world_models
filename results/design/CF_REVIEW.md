# Adversarial review of the counterfactual designs (PCK, PCT, TRS)

All cited arXiv ids fetched through the arXiv API; the paper's text checked with pdftotext (Thm 2.2, Thm 3.6, limits, open directions); the authors' repository still at commit 5c84bba (no follow-up code).

## Bottom line
Each design as written draws a strong "known components" objection. Reviewer's novelty estimates: PCK 0.15, PCT 0.10, TRS 0.12 (designers: 0.35-0.40, 0.28, 0.30-0.35). Decision: conditional GO for one merged algorithm: PCK's cross-world kriging rule with the kernel identified two ways (polarization of the action-dependent variance and persistence through lagged "echo" covariances) plus a factual overidentification test between them. Gate P-A decides between an algorithm paper and a fallback analysis paper. PCT becomes a limitations section or is dropped; TRS becomes the multi-step section.

## Novelty audit
PCK (0.15): for a rank-one disturbance K(a, a') = s(a) s(a') with affine s, so PCK reduces to y' = mu(a') + [s(a')/s(a)](y - mu(a)): a continuity-signed location-scale rule, i.e. the authors' open direction (iii). The pilots compared only against the unsigned sd-ratio (experiments/cf_pilots/additivity/t1.py, t7.py), so the headline lambda = 1 result (0.027 vs 0.991) is a strawman; the authors' gain dial is rank one. For d = 1 the full joint law of random coefficients is identified (Beran-Feuerverger-Hall 1996; Hoderlein-Klemela-Mammen 2010), so Thm 1 at d = 1 is the second-moment shadow of a known result; Hildreth-Houck gives the estimator, BLUP the predictor, 2603.27320 / 2507.12581 the interpolation framing. Only the vector rank >= 2 case is new; its antisymmetric gauge is the rotation the paper's Thm 3.6 lower bound already uses.
PCT (0.10): the anchor variant is a control-function counterfactual with a single-index restriction; the anchor-residual-conditioned predictor equals C(a + r_A) in the population, so the pilot tie is expected (Imbens-Newey 2009; offset-free input-disturbance models, Muske-Badgwell 2002, Pannocchia-Rawlings 2003). On Push-T it amounts to predicting the block from the realized kinematic pusher motion. The blind variant, the only distinctive part, failed. The Wiener-Tauberian argument is standard in Berkson/measurement-error identification.
TRS (0.12): coincides with CF-GPS for a linear-Gaussian residual state-space model; T2 is panel-data identification (Chamberlain; Arellano-Bonhomme 2012; Evdokimov 2010); WhatIf (2611.33745, 25 Nov) already has the mechanism; the paper itself points at repeated observations of a shared disturbance and lists chaining as open.
Follow-ups since 18 Nov: 2611.28350, 2611.29259, 2611.33745, 2611.33764 (building energy, not relevant). None rescales by an identified cross-action kernel or uses persistence to identify the coupling. Nothing found that kills combining polarization and echo identification with a factual overidentification test.

## Soundness
PCK: Thm 1 correct and elementary (only symmetric parts of cross blocks identified). Nugget ambiguity real but not fatal: fresh action-independent noise cannot be distinguished from the shared offset block; N = 0 reproduces residual reuse in additive worlds (the only safe default); setting N from readout error injects an assumption and loses the never-worse-than-residual-reuse property. The variance function is contaminated by action-dependent model error (cross-fitting removes variance, not bias). Thm 2 correct. Thm 3 correct but trivial (best linear predictor; minimax only for linear rules or Gaussian worlds). Thm 4/5 wrong as stated: at lambda = 1 the command is a(1 + 1.59 eps); at a = 0 the gain disturbance is unobservable and ||L*|| blows up, so risk for a' != 0 is order h^2; needs an observability floor or excluding the zero-gain set; h^2 decay not observed numerically: drop or demote. No contradiction with Thm 3.6.
PCT: T1 correct and trivial; the no-injective-readout-under-collapse part is a nice negative result about the authors' direction (ii). T2 correct only for A = R^p, a Lebesgue density and a characteristic function without zeros; fails for bounded action sets without analyticity (the authors' bounded tanh disturbance), for rank-deficient disturbances (confirmed by the Push-T failure) and for gain faults: as stated it says nothing about its target benchmark. T3-T5 conditional on a known map. The channel assumption is "knowledge of where the disturbance enters" (paper, Table 1); the anchor variant uses privileged knowledge of actuated coordinates.
TRS: T1 correct (corollary of Thm 2.2; the useful remark is that additivity fixes the action gauge but not the state gauge). Observation (a) right (sum over horizon O(H^2), independent of h). T2 correct with the similarity freedom; must be cited as panel/SSM identification. T3 standard Kalman algebra. S4 is the paper's coupling assumption; the gain over replay is entirely in the persistent part.

## Experiments
PCK: missing and fatal: the signed location-scale rule (ties on every rank-one cell); benchmark circularity (the authors' dial is exactly affine): misspecified-psi cells needed (saturating gain, quadratic drag, deadzone); add heteroscedastic conditional flow, gain-rescaled input-disturbance abduction, binned Hildreth-Houck BLUP, rho-interpolation tuned on held-out counterfactuals (upper reference), CF-GPS with a learned CVAE SCM; single-seed pilots. PCT: add the offset-free input-disturbance model, CausalInverter, mixture-of-experts plus residual reuse; the best cheap fix must include the control-function predictor. TRS: add CF-GPS with a learned SCM, a WhatIf-style amortized persistent posterior, CaDM, an Arellano-Bonhomme panel moment estimator. Pixels on a T4: feasible for HandReach, FetchReach, Ball-in-Cup (EGL rendering, frozen MAE-B / DINOv2-S); Push-T with a scripted prefix (stated deviation); not PassWater; about 15-25 T4-hours.

## Decision: conditional GO, one merged algorithm, fallback to an analysis paper
Persistence (E[r_t r_{t+1}^T | x_t, x_{t+1}] proportional to K(a_t, a_{t+1})) identifies the off-diagonal cross-action kernel directly, including the antisymmetric component polarization cannot recover. Both routes give identification beyond the rotation gauge, a factual overidentification test (falsification, not certification) and a multi-step extension under the same object. Reviewer's novelty estimate about 0.25-0.30 for Neural Networks / EAAI; about 50% chance of clearing P-A.

Protocol (5 fit seeds; 512 parents x 64 draws x 3 queries; whole-parent bootstrap 95% CIs; Holm across environments; torch.set_num_threads(2), two jobs on 4 cores):
- Stage 0 replication (CPU, about 4 h): authors' HandReach hand, hand_affine50, hand_affine100 at state level; residual reuse at lambda = 1 within +-5 points of the paper, lambda = 0.5 near 70%; stop and fix otherwise.
- Stage 1 honesty check (CPU, about 4 h): PCK versus signed location-scale, unsigned sd-ratio, residual reuse, flow abduction, gain-rescaled input-disturbance on the analytic dial and HandReach dial cells; expected tie with signed location-scale; no headline may use rank-one cells.
- Stage 2 gate P-A (CPU, about 1.5 days): rank >= 2 worlds from the authors' simulators: HandReach actuator_affine with two independent drivers (offset and gain), lambda in {0.5, 1}; Ball-in-Cup gain plus body_force wind; FetchReach gain plus load; misspecified-psi variants (saturating gain, deadzone). Arms: trained predictor; outcome-blind oracle; residual reuse (exact, feature, readout); signed and unsigned location-scale; heteroscedastic affine flow abduction; gain-rescaled input-disturbance; binned Hildreth-Houck BLUP; CF-GPS with CVAE SCM; rho-interpolation tuned on counterfactuals (reference); exact-structure oracle; merged algorithm in polarization-only, echo-only and joint versions. Pass: joint R^2 >= best non-oracle non-tuned baseline + 0.10 with CI excluding 0 in >= 2 of 3 environments, and no worse than residual reuse - 0.02 on misspecified cells. Stop: if P-A fails, no algorithm paper.
- Stage 3 gates P-B, P-C, P-D (CPU, about 1 day): iid_fraction in {0, 0.5, 1} crossed with the dial plus the rotation world Y = (I +- aJ) xi. P-B no harm: additive and hand_load within 1.0 point of residual reuse; iid_fraction = 1 within 1.0 point of polarization-only. P-C test: size <= 0.08 at nominal 0.05 in class; power >= 0.80 on Push-T block velocities, cos fold, misspecified cells (if it fails, drop the test claim). P-D gauge: joint R^2 >= 0.90 on the rotation world with persistence while polarization-only stays near chance.
- Stage 4 gate P-E (CPU, about 1 day): 5-transition episodes on HandReach and Ball-in-Cup with persistent gain/load; arms chained residual reuse, ALPaCA posterior mean, WhatIf-style amortized persistent latent, CaDM, CF-GPS with learned SCM, oracle kernel; pass step-5 R^2 >= best baseline + 0.05 with CI excluding 0, else cut the section.
- Stage 5 gate P-F (T4, about 20 h): HandReach and Ball-in-Cup with frozen MAE-B and DINOv2-S and the paper's readouts; pass if at least 50% of the Stage 2 margin survives, else restrict claims to accurate-state readouts.
- Stage 6 gate P-G (CPU, about 0.5 day): the authors' CEM on rank-2 cells; success >= max(residual reuse, signed location-scale) + 10 points.
Total about 4-5 CPU-days plus about 20 T4-hours; target a submittable draft within five weeks.

Fallback (b): "Where Residual Reuse Breaks: Gauges, Couplings and Cheap Fixes for Counterfactual World Models" (scalar equivalence of PCK and signed location-scale; contact negative results; state-gauge non-identification; control-function predictor as a strong contact baseline; benchmarks on the authors' code); no new-algorithm claim.

If (a) passes: working title "Cross-World Kriging: Identifying Counterfactual Couplings in World Models from Action-Dependent and Persistent Disturbance Statistics". Contributions: amortized cross-world kernel head with kriging transport (contains residual reuse and the outcome-blind predictor); identification: exact second-moment gauge under polarization shown to be the rotation of Thm 3.6, plus persistence removing it; factual overidentification test; persistent-latent smoother for multi-step counterfactuals; experiments on the authors' simulators at state and pixel level. Non-claims: not the first to rescale with the command (rank one equals signed location-scale, the authors' direction (iii)); d = 1 identification is classical random-coefficient theory and the echo route has panel precedents; no contact solution; no factual certification, only falsification; no rate or minimax-separation claim unless verified and with the zero-gain set excluded; not the first persistent-latent counterfactual model (CF-GPS, ALPaCA, WhatIf); fresh versus shared action-independent noise not identified without persistence.
