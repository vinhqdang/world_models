Improving on arXiv 2611.24778. Designer's recommendation: Candidate 1, the twin residual smoother (TRS), candid novelty probability 0.30-0.35 (at the threshold, not clearly above). ## Two structural observations
(a) Chained residual reuse does not inherit the O(h^2) guarantee: replaying r_t at a counterfactual state z'_t leaves a per-step error h[w(z'_t, eps_t) - w(z_t, eps_t)], first order in h; summed over a physical horizon it is about L_w times the integral of ||z' - z||, independent of h. Chaining T short transitions is no better than one long transition; rho_T ~ (d_T / L)^2 with d_T growing along the horizon.
(b) A second gauge: in an additive world, q~(z, eps) = q(z, G_z(eps)) with a state-dependent nu-preserving bijection G_z has the same factual trajectory law and the same exact one-step counterfactuals but a different two-step counterfactual. Additivity fixes the action gauge but not the state gauge; for fresh disturbances the state gauge is not identified by any amount of factual data. For a disturbance component shared across at least two transitions, within-episode pairs are natural paired trials of the same latent at different states and actions, so the factual episode law fixes it.

## Candidate 1 (recommended): twin residual smoother (TRS)
Idea: split the factual residual sequence by cross-time predictability; transport the persistent part (identified coupling) to the counterfactual state-action with a learned operator, and replay the innovation (unidentified coupling) at the matched time index.
Assumptions: coordinates y = readout pi(z); x_t = (y_t, a_t, t); residual r_t = y_{t+1} - mu(x_t) with mu cross-fitted. (S1) r_t = Phi(x_t) xi_t + eta_t, xi in R^m; (S2) xi_{t+1} = A xi_t + omega_t, eta zero-mean independent across t with covariance N; (S3) sequential exchangeability of logged actions; (S4) counterfactual semantics: same xi path and same eta_t (the only uncheckable assumption); (S5) training episodes contain at least 2 transitions sharing the latent (3 if A unknown).
Training: fit mu as in the paper with 5-fold out-of-sample residuals; Phi_theta MLP (2 x 256, SiLU) to R^{p x m}, m = 8 (grid 4/8/16); A = diag(sigmoid(rho)) initialised at 0.97; Q = I - A^2; N = diag(softplus). Loss: exact Gaussian NLL of each episode's residual vector with C[s,t] = Phi(x_s) A^{|t-s|} Phi(x_t)^T + delta_st N (Cholesky or Kalman innovations form; valid under state feedback; naive regression of r_s r_t^T on (x_s, x_t) is not). Jacobian penalty 1e-3 ||dPhi/dx||^2; Adam 1e-3, batch 256 episodes, 100-300 epochs, weight decay 1e-4; select m and penalties by held-out leave-one-time-out NLL.
Hindsight inference: RTS smoother gives xi_hat_{t|T}, Sigma_{t|T}; innovation iota_t = r_t - Phi(x_t) xi_hat_{t|T}; rollout y'_{t+1} = mu(x'_t) + Phi(x'_t) xi_hat_{t|T} + iota_t; 16 posterior draws for uncertainty (zero at a' = a). Forward use k steps ahead: mu(x) + Phi(x) A^k xi_hat_{t|t}. Planner: the paper's CEM (256 candidates, 32 elites, 4 rounds) with hindsight cost J + sd; factual sequence always a candidate.
Reductions: constant Phi or zero persistent variance gives the paper's rule (chained); N = 0 gives the posterior-mean simulator; Phi depending on t only gives replay.
Candid novelty: pieces are known (ALPaCA 1807.08912, Neural-Fly 2205.06908, Kalman/RTS, Abbeel-Quigley-Ng 2006, CF-GPS 1811.06272; in a linear-Gaussian residual state-space model CF-GPS's posterior-mean rollout coincides with TRS: strongest objection). New: the two-gauge analysis, the estimator treating the components differently because of it, the exact risk decomposition against both competitors, the non-composability result, a factual calibration certificate.
Theorems: T1 state-gauge non-identification (proved by construction); T2 persistence identifies K(x, x') = Phi(x) P Phi(x')^T on reachable pairs and N up to a similarity of xi (proof sketch; Gaussian; second moments only otherwise; the fresh coupling is exactly the remaining freedom); T3 exact risk and dominance in the linear-Gaussian case: R_twin = tr(dPhi Sigma_{t|T} dPhi^T), R_replay = R_twin + E||dPhi xi_hat||^2, R_postmean = R_twin + E||iota||^2; with static xi and persistent excitation R_twin <= ||dPhi||^2 / (kappa T); T4 chain bound sum_t lambda^{T-1-t} sqrt(R_twin,t) for a lambda-Lipschitz estimated world; T5 non-composability (proved for a linear-gradient field). Conjectured: minimax lower bound for fresh disturbances with smooth state gauge.

## Kill test (pre-registered)
Stage A (CPU, state coordinates, about one day): E1 authors' HandReach and Ball-in-Cup via native.py with joint_damping, actuator_affine at lambda = 1, body_force; 5-transition episodes; persistent fault with fresh share alpha in {0, 0.5, 1}. E2 boat T=8, L in {2.5, 5, 10, 1000}, drift 0.3, gust share {0, 0.5, 1}. E3 our wall world with a persistent field (stress case only). Independent and local (30 degree) alternative sequences; five seeds; at least 1000 test episodes per cell.
Baselines: chained residual reuse (readout, feature, exact means); trained predictor; k=3 history predictor with chained reuse; oracle without outcome; posterior mean (ALPaCA style); CaDM-style context model; time-only Kalman observer; distance-discounted replay and kNN residual transport; CVAE noise abduction (CF-GPS with a learned SCM); oracle-kernel TRS (ceiling).
Pass (all): (1) E1 with alpha <= 0.5: TRS R2 at step 5 at least 0.25 above the best replay variant and at least 0.05 above the posterior mean at alpha = 0.5, paired bootstrap 95% intervals excluding 0; (2) E2 mixed, L <= 10: TRS risk at most 0.85 of the best of replay, discounted replay and posterior mean; (3) no harm: at L = 1000, alpha = 1 and fully fresh training, TRS within 0.01 relative of replay; (4) planning on E1: hindsight success at least 10 points above the best baseline; forward replanning at least 10 points above reuse-last under persistence and at most 3 points below the plain predictor under fresh and none; (5) calibration: leave-one-time-out standardised residual variance in [0.8, 1.25]. Stop if (1) or (3) fails.
Stage B (Colab T4): frozen MAE-B and DINOv2-S with the paper's readouts on E1; pass if at least half of the Stage A margin survives.
